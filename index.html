Charlotte County, New Brunswick · 1878–1880

The New River Murder — a timeline

Thomas Edward Ward disappeared from his New River boarding house on September 9, 1878. Thomas Dowd was hanged for his murder at St. Andrews on January 14, 1879; Eliza Ward was condemned with him, reprieved, and freed six weeks later. Assembled from newspaper reports of the time and two later research pieces held at the Charlotte County Archives.
